# Setup

What a deployment admin needs before launching it, and what to do after.

## Before launching

- The account or key the vendor issues, and where in the vendor's own
  settings it is found.
- Anything to arrange with the vendor first.

## After launching

1. Open the plugin's Settings and enter each setting it asks for.
2. Link the external accounts it reports, on its Account links page, if it
   has one.
3. Check its Summary reads healthy.
